∫ Monte Carlo Transfer Feasibility Formulation
Let interchange junction be $J$, with incoming Leg 1 and outgoing Leg 2.
Scheduled buffer: $\Delta t = T_{\text{dep}}(L_2) - T_{\text{arr}}(L_1)$.

Transfer succeeds in trial $k$ if:
D_{1,k} - 0.3·D_{2,k} ≤ Δt - τ_walk Where D is Log-Normal sampled delay and τ_walk is platform transit
We run $N = 10,000$ iterations using empirical log-normal delay distributions calibrated to 90-day NTES punctuality. Any corridor with $P(\text{Success}) < 0.85$ is pruned.

📜 Railway Board Gazette Refund Rules
  • Train Cancelled by Railways: Rule 6(b) mandates 100% full refund auto-credited to source bank account for online e-tickets without cancellation fees.
  • Missed Connection via Late Running: Under PRS Rule 54 (linked PNRs), passenger is entitled to full refund on the connecting ticket without cancellation charges.
